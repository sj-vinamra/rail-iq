Method & assumptions

Phase 1 screening model for trains on a straight, uniform section, with receptors at a perpendicular distance from the track centre. All reference values are inputs and must be reviewed by an acoustic specialist.

Airborne noise

  • Pass-by SEL: SEL = SEL_ref + 10 log Ncars + K_v log(v / v_ref) + adj − K_d log(d / d_ref) − IL, with K_d = 10 (hard ground) or 15 (soft ground) for a line source.
  • Period levels from the number of pass-bys: LAeq,T = SEL + 10 log N − 10 log T; Ldn = 10 log[(15·10^(Ld/10) + 9·10^((Ln+10)/10)) / 24].
  • Approximate pass-by maximum: Lmax ≈ SEL − 10 log(train length / speed), valid close to the track.
  • Barrier: Maekawa, IL = 10 log(3 + 20 N) with N = 2δ / λ at 500 Hz, capped at 20 dB; no loss if the barrier does not break the line of sight.

Vibration

  • Lv = Lv_ref + 20 log(v / v_ref) + adj_track + adj_building − 20 n log(d / d_ref) − 8.686 α (d − d_ref) (geometric spreading and material damping).
  • Ground-borne noise LA = Lv − K with K = 35, 50 or 55 dB depending on the vibration spectrum.

Not in Phase 1

Curved track and wheel squeal, multiple sources (fans, stations), existing ambient noise and relative impact criteria, frequency spectra, building floor amplification, and detailed vibration propagation from measurements. These need a detailed assessment.
